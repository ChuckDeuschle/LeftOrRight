# Cards

Cards are the medium of the combat loop. Each card has three abilities: **Left**, **Right**, and **Combo**.

## Card Abilities

### Left
The effect that resolves when the card is swiped left — the left-hand cast.

### Right
The effect that resolves when the card is swiped right — the right-hand cast.

### Combo
A bonus effect that fires when this card is played in the **opposite** direction of the previously played card. If the last card was played left and you swipe this one right, the combo triggers (and vice versa).

## Example

> **Mage Bolt** — Left: 5 dmg • Right: 5 shield • Combo: +2 shield

Played right after a left-played card: the player gets the 5 shield from Right *and* the +2 shield from Combo, for 7 shield this swipe.

## Why Combo Exists

Without it, the optimal play can degrade into committing to one side — especially when stacked with the Gambler archetype, where one-sided play accrues a guaranteed crit. Combo pulls in the opposite direction: alternation gets its own reward, and pure ping-pong play has to be balanced against the cost of leaving one side's deck-pressure unbuilt.

## Open Design Questions

### Chain vs. Reset
- **Chain** — Continuous alternation triggers a combo on every card after the first. Strongest play is perfect zigzag, every swipe.
- **Reset** — Combo fires once, then both sides must be played again before another combo can trigger. You have to "earn" each combo, which discourages mindless alternation.

### Flat Bonus vs. Card Improvement
- **Flat bonus** — Combo is a one-shot extra on that swipe ("+2 shield this play"). Static across the fight.
- **Card improvement** — Each successful combo permanently upgrades the card for the remainder of the fight ("+1 to both damage and shield"). Snowballs over time, making alternation a long-term build strategy rather than a per-swipe tactic.
