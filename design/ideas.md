## Low-Health Bonuses

In most games with HP, only the last point matters — you're either alive or dead, and being at 100% HP vs. 1% HP is mechanically identical. Could be more interesting to grant **escalating bonuses** as the player's HP drops past thresholds (e.g., a bonus at 50% HP, another at 25% HP, etc.).

The framing flip is the key: instead of penalties at low health (which create death spirals), low health becomes a **resource** — you take risks to reach the bonus tiers. Comeback potential and risk-reward decisions emerge.

**Open questions:**
- What kind of bonus? Flat damage/shield modifiers, card cost reductions, new abilities, modified Combo behavior?
- Active only while below the threshold, or permanent for the rest of the encounter once unlocked?
