# Encounter Archetypes

Every encounter uses the [core combat loop](../core-loop.md) — enemy intent, countdown, deck uncertainty. These archetypes layer additional mechanics on top to create varied combat experiences.

---

## Requeue Enemy

Attacks shift your card order. Left-to-top / right-to-bottom repositioning becomes an enemy-imposed effect rather than a player choice. The enemy disrupts your deck rhythm: a block card you were counting on seeing soon gets pushed to the bottom.

**Adds:** anticipation of deck state disruption, pressure to play cards before the enemy reorders them.

---

## Infiltrator

Shuffles disruption cards into your deck. Hitting one triggers an immediate enemy action or debuff. Some variants warn 1–2 cards ahead, giving a narrow window to prepare.

**Adds:** memory and prediction — across repeated plays, you learn where the traps tend to land.

---

## Mirror

Rewards or punishes directional chains. Consecutive same-direction swipes build a bonus; breaking the chain either empowers the enemy or weakens your next play.

**Adds:** rhythm and sequencing. The enemy warps what "a good swipe" means for the duration of the fight.

---

## Enraged Boss

A rage meter fills with each card played. When it fills, the boss acts and hits harder than a scheduled intent would. You can voluntarily end your turn early to fight the boss at lower rage, trading actions for safety.

**Adds:** a second resource to manage alongside the intent countdown. Boss-tier tension.

Design variants:
- Rage resets each turn vs. carries over (escalating pressure)
- Some cards reduce rage (tension relief)
- Rage threshold scales as the fight progresses
